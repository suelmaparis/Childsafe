Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Introduction

ChildSafe is a digital child protection initiative designed to support the reporting, assessment and review of potential online exposure of children and other child-safety concerns. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored and protected when you use our platform.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use ChildSafe, we may process:

  • Report information, including the social media platform, URL, reason for the report and description provided.
  • Report status, timestamps and review or audit records.
  • Account information needed to authenticate authorized administrators and reviewers.
  • Technical information needed to protect the service, prevent abuse and maintain security.

Please do not submit unnecessary personal information or upload images of children. Provide only the information needed to describe and report the concern.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Receive, document and review child-safety reports.
  • Assess and prioritize potential risks.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the platform.
  • Manage authorized access and maintain audit records.
  • Operate integrations that have been explicitly enabled.

4. Automated Analysis and Third-Party Services

When AI-assisted risk assessment is enabled, relevant report information may be processed by OpenAI's API to assist with analysis. Automated assessments are support tools and do not replace appropriate human review.

ChildSafe uses hosting and database infrastructure to operate the service. If integrations with Meta platforms are enabled, information may also be processed through those integrations in accordance with the applicable permissions and terms. These providers process information as necessary to provide their services and subject to their applicable policies.

Website Analytics and Cookies

ChildSafe may use Google Analytics 4, a website analytics service provided by Google, to understand how visitors access and use our public website.

Analytics information may include page visits, approximate geographic location, device and browser information, and how visitors reach our website, including through social media.

Google Analytics is not intended to collect the contents of child-safety reports, reported URLs, report descriptions or personal information about children.

Where required, analytics cookies and similar technologies will be used only after the visitor has provided consent. Visitors may decline optional analytics tracking.

For more information, please consult Google's Privacy Policy .

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information may be accessed by authorized personnel or service providers when necessary to operate, secure and maintain ChildSafe. We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to address security or safety concerns.

6. Data Storage and Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including report review, security, recordkeeping and applicable legal obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the type and purpose of the information.

7. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration or disclosure. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

ChildSafe is intended for adults and authorized personnel who report or review child-safety concerns. It is not intended to invite children to create accounts or submit personal information. We seek to minimize the collection of children's personal information.

9. Your Requests and Choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with you, request correction or request deletion. We will review such requests in accordance with applicable law and any legitimate safeguarding, security or recordkeeping requirements.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as ChildSafe develops. The latest version and its effective date will be published on this page.

11. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data-related requests, contact: suelmaparis@hotmail.com

Política de Privacidade — Português

O ChildSafe é uma iniciativa de proteção digital infantil destinada a apoiar a comunicação e análise de possíveis situações de exposição de crianças na internet.

Podemos processar informações fornecidas nas denúncias, como plataforma, URL, motivo e descrição, bem como dados necessários à segurança e administração do serviço. Quando a análise assistida por inteligência artificial estiver ativa, informações relevantes poderão ser processadas pela API da OpenAI.

Não vendemos informações pessoais. Os dados podem ser processados por prestadores de serviços necessários ao funcionamento da plataforma. Conservamos as informações pelo período razoavelmente necessário para análise, segurança e cumprimento de obrigações legais.

O ChildSafe poderá utilizar o Google Analytics 4 para compreender quantas pessoas visitam o site público, de que países vêm e como chegam à plataforma, incluindo através das redes sociais.

Esta ferramenta não se destina a recolher o conteúdo das denúncias, os links denunciados, as descrições dos casos ou informações pessoais sobre crianças.

Quando legalmente necessário, a utilização de cookies e tecnologias semelhantes para fins estatísticos dependerá do consentimento do visitante, que poderá recusar esta recolha opcional.

Para questões de privacidade ou pedidos relacionados com os teus dados, contacta suelmaparis@hotmail.com .
