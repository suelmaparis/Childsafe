Public Reporting

Help protect a child online

Report public online content that may expose, identify or endanger a child.

✓
You can report anonymously

We do not ask for your name or email. Submit only the link to the public content and information needed to explain your concern.

Protect the child while reporting

Do not download, screenshot, copy or upload images or videos of the child to ChildSafe. Please submit the original public link instead.

Immediate danger?

If you believe a child is in immediate physical danger, contact the appropriate emergency or child-protection authorities in addition to submitting this report.

Report details

Tell us where you found the content and why you believe it may place a child at risk.

ChildSafe will assess the report and may prioritise it for human review based on child-safety risk indicators.
