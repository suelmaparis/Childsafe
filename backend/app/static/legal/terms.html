These Terms of Service apply to your use of the ChildSafe website and reporting tools. By using the service, you agree to use it responsibly and in line with these Terms and applicable law.

1. Purpose and scope

ChildSafe is a child online-safety project intended to help receive, organize, and review reports about potential online exposure of children and related safety concerns. Features may change as the project develops.

2. Responsible reporting

You agree to submit information honestly and in good faith, provide only details relevant to the concern, and avoid knowingly false, harassing, or malicious reports. Do not use ChildSafe to threaten, harass, impersonate, dox, or target another person.

Where possible, provide a public URL and a factual description rather than copying or redistributing harmful content. Do not upload or send sexual images or videos of children, or any material that would be unlawful to possess or distribute. Do not submit passwords or unrelated sensitive information.

3. No guarantee of outcome

ChildSafe may help organize information for review, but it cannot guarantee that a report will be verified, that content will be removed, or that a platform, law-enforcement agency, or other organization will take action. AI-assisted outputs, when available, are preliminary aids and may be incomplete or incorrect.

4. Prohibited use

  • Attempting to access accounts, systems, or data without authorization.
  • Submitting knowingly false reports or using the service to retaliate against someone.
  • Distributing illegal content, sexual material involving children, malware, or information intended to facilitate harm.
  • Interfering with service availability, bypassing access controls, or misusing reviewer/admin features.
  • Using information obtained through ChildSafe for harassment, discrimination, vigilantism, or unrelated purposes.

5. Privacy

Our handling of information is described in the Privacy Policy. You can request deletion by following the Data Deletion Instructions. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate safety, security, audit, or legal reasons.
