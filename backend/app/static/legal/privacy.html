Effective date: October 12, 2026 · Last updated: October 12, 2026

ChildSafe is a project focused on helping people report and review potential online exposure of children and other child-safety concerns. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be handled when you use the ChildSafe website and reporting tools, why it is used, when it may be shared, and how to request deletion.

Safety note: When possible, submit the public post or page URL and a factual description rather than downloading or redistributing harmful content. Do not send passwords or unrelated sensitive information. Never use ChildSafe to distribute sexual images or videos of children.

1. Information we may collect

  • Report details: platform name, public URL, report category or reason, description, report identifier, status, and the date/time a report was received.
  • Evidence and review records: information or files you choose to submit where the relevant feature is available, and notes, decisions, actions, or audit records created by authorized reviewers.
  • Contact information: an email address or other contact details if you provide them in a form or by contacting ChildSafe.
  • Authorized reviewer account information: account identifiers and security-related records needed to authenticate and administer authorized access.
  • Technical and security data: service logs, timestamps, request metadata, and information needed to prevent abuse, investigate errors, and protect the service.
  • Integration data: if a platform integration is enabled and authorized, information returned by that integration may include public content URLs or identifiers, account/page identifiers, timestamps, and relevant safety signals. ChildSafe does not ask for your Facebook or Instagram password.

The information processed depends on the features actually enabled and what a person chooses to submit. Please provide only information relevant to the safety concern.

2. How we use information

  • Receive, organize, assess, and follow up on reports about potential child-safety risks online.
  • Support human review, moderation, and safety-related decisions.
  • Maintain report status, reviewer actions, audit trails, and service reliability.
  • Detect misuse, protect accounts and systems, and investigate security incidents.
  • Respond to questions, deletion requests, and other communications.
  • Meet legal obligations and comply with applicable platform terms and policies.

3. AI-assisted processing and service providers

Where AI-assisted analysis is enabled, relevant report text, URLs, and associated metadata may be processed by the configured AI service provider to help categorize, summarize, or prioritize reports. AI output is an aid for review, not a final determination that content is illegal or that a platform must remove it. We aim to limit processing to information relevant to the report.

ChildSafe may rely on service providers for hosting, databases, security, and AI-assisted processing. They may process information only as needed to provide or protect the service, subject to their applicable terms and safeguards. The current website is hosted through Render; an AI provider may be used when the AI feature is active.

4. How information may be shared

ChildSafe does not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to operate the service, assess a report, protect a child or another person, comply with law, respond to valid legal process, address security abuse, or work with a relevant platform or competent authority. Any sharing should be limited to what is relevant and appropriate for the purpose.

Submitting a report does not guarantee that a platform will remove content or that an authority will take action.

5. Retention

Reports and related records may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to assess and document a safety concern, prevent repeated abuse, maintain security and audit records, improve service reliability, or meet legal obligations. When information is no longer needed, ChildSafe will take reasonable steps to delete it or remove identifying details, subject to applicable legal and safety-related retention needs.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, or unrelated personal information. Access to reviewer and administrative information should be restricted to authorized people.

7. Children’s privacy

ChildSafe’s purpose is to support child safety. The reporting and review tools are not intended to encourage children to create accounts or disclose personal details. Reporters should avoid including a child’s name, address, school, or other identifying details unless genuinely necessary to describe the concern.

8. Your choices and deletion requests

You can request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with you or a report, subject to applicable law and legitimate safety, security, or recordkeeping needs. To request deletion, follow the Data Deletion Instructions. We may need enough information to identify the relevant record and verify the request, but please do not email sensitive images of children.

9. International processing

Service providers may process information in the United States or other locations where they operate. Where required, appropriate safeguards should be applied to international transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as ChildSafe’s features or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

11. Contact

Privacy and data requests: suelmaparis@hotmail.com.

Política de Privacidade (Português)

Data de entrada em vigor: 12 de outubro de 2026.

O ChildSafe é um projeto destinado a ajudar na comunicação e análise de possíveis riscos de segurança infantil na Internet. Podemos tratar dados incluídos em denúncias, como plataforma, URL pública, motivo, descrição, identificador e estado da denúncia; dados de contacto fornecidos voluntariamente; registos técnicos e de segurança; e registos de revisão efetuada por pessoas autorizadas.

Usamos estes dados para receber e analisar denúncias, apoiar a revisão humana, manter registos de segurança, prevenir abusos, responder a pedidos e cumprir obrigações legais. Quando a análise assistida por inteligência artificial estiver ativa, texto, URLs e metadados relevantes poderão ser processados pelo fornecedor configurado para ajudar a classificar ou resumir denúncias. A análise automática não substitui uma avaliação humana nem garante a remoção de conteúdo.

O ChildSafe não vende dados pessoais. Poderá partilhar informação estritamente necessária com fornecedores que alojam ou protegem o serviço, com plataformas relevantes ou autoridades competentes quando necessário e permitido por lei. Os registos são conservados pelo tempo razoavelmente necessário para analisar denúncias, proteger crianças, manter a segurança e cumprir obrigações legais. Podes pedir acesso, correção ou eliminação de dados através da página Instruções para Eliminação de Dados ou pelo email suelmaparis@hotmail.com.

Quando possível, envia o endereço público da publicação e uma descrição factual, em vez de descarregar ou redistribuir conteúdo prejudicial. Nunca uses o ChildSafe para distribuir imagens ou vídeos sexuais de crianças. Esta política poderá ser atualizada à medida que o serviço evoluir.
